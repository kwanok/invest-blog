## TL;DR

- Major equity indices are rebounding, signaling a short-term recovery in risk appetite.
- With key macro events ahead, **risk control matters more than momentum chasing**.
- Keep execution mechanical: **60% max exposure** and hard loss guards (**-1.5% daily / -4% weekly**).

## Key Snapshot

- Yahoo Finance index board shows broad strength across US/Europe/Asia major indices.
- VIX appears in a declining range on the same board, consistent with short-term risk-on sentiment.
- KRWUSD on Stooq is slightly higher vs prior session (KRW weakness), so KRW-based investors should monitor FX risk alongside equity signals.

> Data is a 2026-02-09 KST midday snapshot; intraday values may change.

## Signal Dashboard

### Watch signals
- Whether index rebound continues
- Whether VIX downtrend holds
- Whether KRWUSD extends higher (further KRW weakness)

### Warning triggers (pause/reduce new risk)
- **VIX breaks above 20**
- **KRWUSD spikes sharply**
- Volatility re-expands into key event windows

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] **No chase entries**: prefer pullback confirmation over opening-gap momentum entries
- [ ] **Track FX + volatility together**: if KRW weakens further and vol rises, cut high-beta exposure
- [ ] **Pre-event de-risking**: keep portfolio posture conservative ahead of major macro catalysts

## Risk Rules (Fixed Numbers)

### Capital allocation
- **Max total exposure: 60%**
- **Scale-in plan: 20% → 20% → 20% (total 60%)**
- No new entries above 60%

### Entry triggers
- Indexes: scale in only on pullback near 20-day average / prior support (no breakout chase)
- Risk filter: pause new entries if VIX rises (e.g., above 20) or KRWUSD jumps

### Loss limits
- **Single-position stop: -5%**
- **Daily portfolio loss guard: stop new trades at -1.5%**
- **Weekly drawdown guard: cut risk assets by half at -4%**
